# FalconMail Privacy Policy (draft)

FalconMail is a desktop email client. It runs entirely on your Mac.

- Your email, contacts, calendar events and files are transferred directly
  between your Mac and your mail or cloud provider (for example Google). They
  are never sent to the FalconMail developers or any third party.
- Sign-in tokens and passwords are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.
- Archives you create are stored where you choose: your own Google Drive,
  OneDrive, Nextcloud, or a local folder. Optional archive encryption uses a
  password that is never stored.
- FalconMail contacts GitHub to check for new versions. That request contains
  no personal data.
- FalconMail collects no analytics and has no servers of its own.

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to display and
manage your mailbox, contacts, calendar and archive files inside FalconMail,
in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the
Limited Use requirements.

Contact: replace with the support email used on the Google consent screen.
